# One More Round - lyrics

Theme (never named): the last night of a summer fair. The two of them on the wheel while the town packs up, the bulbs
going out from the bottom, asking the operator - and each other - for one more turn. Images: sugar, used-up tickets,
the generator, the sky being folded away, bulbs, the bar of the gondola, stars "on the house".

Hook line: **"One more round"** (3 words, open "ow" of round on the long peak note C5 / Eb5), repeated and chopped.
Punchy lines: "all the stars are on the house", "generator humming like it's had enough", "spin us up where the dark
can't reach".

## verse (bars 17-24, AABB-ish slant: up / enough, sky / goodbye)

| line | syllables | rhyme |
|---|---|---|
| Sugar on my fingers, tickets all used up, | 11 | A |
| generator humming like it's had enough, | 11 | A |
| someone's folding up the sky, | 7 | B |
| one bulb at a time, goodbye. | 7 | B |

## breakdown (pre-hook, bars 25-28)

| Hold the bar, don't look down, | 6 | A |
| all the stars are on the house. | 7 | A (slant: down / house) |

## build (hook) x2

One more round, one more round, one more round, oh. / One more round, one more round, one more round.

## chorus (breakdown 2, drop 2; each x2)

| all the stars are on the house. | 7 | A' (the pre-hook line returns as the payoff) |

## talk box (TIGER mouths it, the guitar speaks it)

Round and round, ooh wah, round we go. (x2) - moving vowels (ooh -> wah) for the box's sweep.

Notes: stresses on strong beats (SU-gar, FIN-gers, TICK-ets, GEN-er-a-tor, HUM-ming, e-NOUGH, FOLD-ing); open vowels
on the long notes (up, nough, sky, bye, down, house, round, oh); "our" was two syllables in CMUdict (aw-er) -> "my".
The one direct line is "goodbye" (the verse's turn). `out/lyrics.txt` goes along with the mp3.
