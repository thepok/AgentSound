# Lanterns Along the River - notes

Set 2 of the hand-written new-age references (mood: hopeful). A-flat major, 4/4, 88 BPM (84-92), ~3:15, feel
`bright`.

- **Form**: RONDO - intro (2) | refrain A1 (8) | episode B1 (12) | refrain A2 (8) | episode B2 (6) + build (4) +
  climax (4) | retransition inter (2) | refrain A3 (10) | coda (8).
- **Harmony**: refrain Ab - Eb/G - Fm7 - Db(add9) | Ab/C - Db - Bbm7 Eb7sus4 - Ab; episode 1 in F minor via Db and Gb
  (bVII); episode 2 in C# minor (= Db minor) sinking into E major (bVI); the climax's G#6 is re-heard as A-flat on
  the way home (Ab/Eb - Eb7sus4 - Eb7).
- **Theme**: Eb5 Ab5 C6, a quarter across the beat, the leap to Eb6: a syncopated, bright refrain.
- **Tells avoided**: the climax is a new tune in another key (not the refrain 8va); every refrain return is changed
  (higher start, deceptive close, middle register); this piece deliberately closes its last return on the TONIC
  (ret_tonic 1) - a rondo goes home, the old references all closed deceptively.
