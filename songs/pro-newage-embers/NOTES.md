# Embers, Late - notes

Set 2 of the hand-written new-age references (mood: elegy). C minor (one variation in C major), 4/4, 58 BPM (52-62),
~4:15, feel `gentle`.

- **Form**: PASSACAGLIA - an 8-bar ground (C Eb F G Ab F D G) seven times: var 1-4 (A1 A2 B1 A3), var 5 build, var 6
  climax, var 7 half (A4: it stops on G), coda (4). No intro.
- **Harmony**: the bass never changes, the chords above it always do (Cm Eb Fm Gm ... / Cm(add9) Cm/Eb Fm7 Cm/G
  Abmaj7 F7 Bb/D Gsus4 / C Ebmaj7 F G Abmaj7 Fm6 Dm7 G7 / ... / Abmaj7#11, Bdim7/D): chord_rep4 0.0 despite the ground.
- **Melody**: new over every statement - low long notes, 8ths an octave up, a consolation in major, a syncopated line,
  climbing 8ths, three-voice chords.
- **Tells avoided**: the ground is not the lament tetrachord; the climax is late (~80 %) on the ground's A-flat and
  the top Ab6 is struck once; the last statement breaks off instead of a stock coda; the left hand texture changes
  every variation (held 10ths, lilt, flow, syncopated, 16ths, octaves, open).
