Radio Heart  (original lyric; theme: a late-night drive, a station fading in and out - longing, never named)

[Verse / hook - 8 bars, Am F C G x2]
Paper moon over the parking lot,
I can feel the signal, I can feel it drop.
Turn it up, turn it up, all the static in my heart,
we were made of radio and falling apart.

Hook: "Turn it up, turn it up" (line 3, on the C5 peaks).
Punch line: "we were made of radio and falling apart".
Rhymes: AABB (lot / drop, heart / apart).


Box Talk  (original; what the talk box says - short words with open, moving vowels for the box to shape)

[Talk - 4 bars, Em7 A7 x2]
Talk to me, talk to me, say it slow.
Ooh wah, let the box do the talking.

"Ooh wah": the vowel sweep (oo -> ah) that makes a talk box sound like a wah pedal.
