# Rain on the Skylight - notes

Set 2 of the hand-written new-age references (mood: rain). F minor, 6/8, dotted quarter 42, ~3:30, feel `gentle`.

- **Form**: ternary with the climax in the middle - intro (4) | A1 (16) | B1 (12, 4+4+4) | build (4) | climax (6) |
  inter (4) | A2 (14) | coda (6). The storm comes before the return; the return is the quiet after it.
- **Harmony**: A in F minor with the half-diminished ii and the Neapolitan Gbmaj7; B in D-flat (VI) turning to
  B-flat minor and F7; the build a rising chromatic bass Bb - B - C onto the dominant pedal; the climax Db/C - Bbm/C -
  Ab/C over that pedal. A2 reharmonised (Fm/Eb, the Neapolitan earlier), closing deceptively on Dbmaj7; the coda ends
  in F MAJOR.
- **Theme**: C5 F5 Eb5 C5 - a leap from the 5th to the root, heads on chord tones; range 17 semitones in A1.
- **Tells avoided**: no left-hand-alone intro (the right hand opens alone); the climax is not the theme in chords
  (16th cascades over a pedal); no late climax; the return is reharmonised, starts on a pickup, ends deceptively; the
  coda is not the intro's chord row (Bbm6 drops, the head, Picardy F).
