EXPENSIVE HABITS
(AgentSound - Night Economy)

[Verse 1]
Taxi at the corner at a quarter to four,
you pay with a card that's not yours any more.
The doorman knows your name but he says it all wrong,
we're famous on the guest list where we never belong.

[Pre-chorus]
Nobody here is counting,
nobody here is old,
we smile for the cameras
then we do what we're told.

[Chorus]
Expensive habits,
and a cheap view of the sky,
we spend the night like money,
and the money like time.

[Verse 2]
Your flat has a view of a better flat,
a plant you forgot and a borrowed cat.
We read about houses like poems in bed,
we can't afford the morning, so we dance instead.

[Pre-chorus]
Nobody here is sorry,
nobody here is cold,
we pose for the cameras
then we do what we're told.

[Chorus]
Expensive habits,
and a cheap view of the sky,
we spend the night like money,
and the money like time.

[Bridge]
All the windows on the water
show a party we don't know,
I'll trade it all for one slow Sunday,
the phone switched off and the radio low.

[Chorus - a whole step up]
Expensive habits,
and a cheap view of the sky,
we spend the night like money,
and the money like time.

[Chorus]
Expensive habits,
and a cheap view of the sky,
we spend the night like money,
and we're running out of time.

[Outro]
Expensive habits.
Expensive habits.

Lead vocal: TIGER (DiffSinger voicebank, non-commercial). Backing "ahs": Hoshino Hanami (Lotte V).
