# Expensive Habits - lyrics

**Theme (one sentence)**: two people in a city at night living a life they cannot pay for - and both of them know it.
Circled with urban images (the 4 a.m. taxi, a card that isn't yours, the doorman who gets your name wrong, a view of a
better flat, property pages read like poetry), ironic and cool; one direct line in the bridge.

**Hook line**: "Expensive habits" - the chorus's first line, the title, on the pickup leap to the chorus's top note
(Eb4; F4 after the key change). It returns in every chorus and twice in the outro tag.

**Quotable lines** (the attitude): "we're famous on the guest list where we never belong", "we can't afford the morning,
so we dance instead", "we spend the night like money, and the money like time".

**Direct lines** (deliberate): "I'll trade it all for one slow Sunday" (the bridge's turn) and the last chorus's
"and we're running out of time".
