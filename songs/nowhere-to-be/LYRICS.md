# Nowhere to Be - lyrics (roles/lyricist.md)

**Theme in one sentence**: two people who have nowhere to be - a late night sliding into a slow morning, the world
asked to wait - told only through what they let go of (the clock, the coffee, the phone, the sunrise); the one plain
line comes in the bridge.

**Hook line** (= title, on the hook's held C5): "Nowhere to be". It opens and closes every chorus, returns in the tag,
and turns once, at the very end of the last chorus: "nowhere but you".

**Sparse by design** (HUMAN_FEEDBACK "Vocals": fewer words on longer notes, breathing space): 20 syllables per verse,
18 per chorus, 15 in the bridge - about 0.8 syllables per second inside the phrases, no syllable on an 8th-note
grid run (every syllable step is a quarter or longer, or a single 8th between long notes), a breath (1-2 beats) after
every line. Open vowels on the long notes: wall, cold, glass, call, chair, go, hair, be, go, slow, days, trains,
down, stayed, you.
