# The Long Way Home - notes

Set 2 of the hand-written new-age references (mood: nostalgic / homecoming). E-flat major -> G-flat major, 4/4, 66 BPM
(62-70), ~3:30, feel `gentle`.

- **Form**: song form AABA + an overshooting climax - intro (2) | A1 (8) | A2 (8) | B1 bridge (8) | A3 (8) | build (4)
  | climax (4) | A4 in G-flat (6) | coda (4). The piece ends a minor third higher than it began.
- **Harmony**: Eb - Abmaj7 - Cm7 - Bb7sus4; A2 with Gm7 (iii) and a secondary Eb7/Db; the bridge in C minor (Cm - Ab -
  Fm7 - G7); A3 reharmonised (Gm7 - C7 - Fm7); the climax takes Db7 where Bb7 is expected and lands in G-flat; A4
  closes on Ebm (the old tonic as the new relative minor).
- **Theme**: a SCALE head Bb4 C5 D5 Eb5 into a leap to G5 - step-wise, not an arpeggio.
- **Tells avoided**: the climax is a harmonic event (the turn to G-flat), not the theme 8va; the bridge has its own
  rhythm (long notes on beat 2, a syncopated left hand); the intro is one rolled chord. (ret_tonic reads 1 here because
  the metric compares the root with the starting key only: Ebm in G-flat counts as "the tonic".)
