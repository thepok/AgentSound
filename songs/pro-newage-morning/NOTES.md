# Variations on a Morning - notes

Set 2 of the hand-written new-age references (mood: morning). G major with the Lydian C#, 4/4, 76 BPM (66-82),
~3:30, feel `flowing`.

- **Form**: theme and variations, NO intro - theme (8) | var 1 diminution (8) | var 2 G minor, slower (8) | var 3 in
  triplets (8) | var 4a build (4) + 4b climax (8) | var 5 hymn (8) | coda (6).
- **Harmony**: G - A/G (the Lydian II) - Em7 - Cmaj7 | G/B - Cmaj7 - Am7 D7sus4 - G; var 2 maps it to Gm - Ebmaj7 -

  line in three-voice chords peaking once on A6 and falling by step for six bars, not the theme 8va; the left hand
  changes texture with every variation (quarters, flow, held 10ths, triplets, 16ths, open).
- **Note**: as variations share the theme's harmony by design, climax_chart is high (0.62) - a property of the form.
