Exit Nine  (original lyric; theme: walking out on a whole life overnight - circled, never named)

[Verse]
Keys on the counter, coffee still hot,
dog in the back seat, he don't ask why.
Sold the TV for a tank of gas,
honked twice at the county line.

[Chorus]
All the way to Exit Nine,
tore the mirror off, threw it in the pines.
All the way to Exit Nine -
radio's loud and the dog don't mind.

Hook: "All the way to Exit Nine" (chorus lines 1 + 3, on the A4 peaks).
Punch lines: "Sold the TV for a tank of gas", "tore the mirror off, threw it in the pines".
Rhymes: verse XAXA (why / line), chorus AAAA (Nine / pines / Nine / mind).
