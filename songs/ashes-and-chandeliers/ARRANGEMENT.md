# ARRANGEMENT - Ashes and Chandeliers

## Frame

- 19 sections, 132 bars, ~5:43 of music + a 9 s tail (fermatas included). Four parts, each with its own key, tempo and
  meter (tempo map: rubato, ritardandi, an accelerando, three fermatas).
- Straight feel everywhere (no swing). Meter 4/4, the masque in 3/4.

## The hook

The curtain motif (4 notes): an 8th upbeat on the 5th, a sixth up to a held minor 3rd, then a sigh down by step
(G4 - Eb5 - D5 - C5). The 8-bar theme answers it higher (G4 - Eb5 - G5) and cadences first on the dominant, then on
the tonic. It grows through the piece:

1. intro: hinted, very soft (piano).
2. theme / theme2 / return: the piano states it, harmonized (pianist.arrange ballad: thirds, sixths, close, octaves).
3. calls: the male choir calls it in E minor (B3 - G4 - F#4 - E4), the women answer it higher.
4. masque: in G major on the flutes (D5 - B5 - A5), then again in E minor (B4 - G5 - F#5).
5. anthem / anthem2: the guitar orchestra plays it in A minor, 2 voices in thirds, then 3.
6. solo: opens with it (E5 - C6 - B5 - A5).
7. finale: the major transformation (G4 - E5, a major sixth) - violins in octaves, choir, horns, guitars.
8. summit: sequenced up (Eb5 - C6, F5 - D6) to the peak.
9. coda: the minor motif alone on the piano, then the Picardy chord.

## Transitions

- intro -> theme: the G7 half cadence, the pedal lifts, the upbeat G4.
- theme2 -> middle: the tonic cadence, then a step into Eb (relative major).
- middle -> return: Fm6/Ab - G7 (dominant) back to C minor.
- return -> stab: ritardando, the Ab chord swells (tremolo violins + horns + timpani roll), fermata, general pause,
  then the E minor staccato stab (chromatic mediant shock).
- calls -> masque: tutti stab on B7, stop, the waltz enters pp.
- masque -> ascent: the B7 bar; the B pedal starts under the first choir entry.
- ascent -> riff: accelerando, E major tutti fermata, the drummer's 1-beat pickup.
- solo -> riff2: the harmony guitars answer the last two bars, straight into the riff.
- riff2 -> break -> finale: band hits (F, E), the drummer's end hit, timpani roll, rit. 138 -> 96; the finale's
  downbeat with crash + bass drum + timpani.
- summit -> fall: the last chord with the cymbal roll and the hall raised (orch.ring), then the tam-tam and subito pp.
- fall -> coda: the piano enters alone on the motif.

## Players

- **Piano** (hero piano): `pianist.arrange` style 'ballad' for every melody (intro, theme, theme2, middle, return,
  coda: devices close / octave / thirds / sixths / drop2), one `pianist.Memory` for the song (its big figure saved
  for the coda's cadence, `mem.save(coda.bar(4))`), touch() 40-108 on the melodies; the left hand is a written
  broken-chord ballad pattern (8ths: root, 5th, octave, 10th, ...; 1 and 3 leaning in, a 4-bar arc, +-6 %);
  pedal = `arr.pedal(prog, at)`; rolled chords in intro (55 ms), the Ab swell (60 ms), the summit (40 ms) and the
  final chord (85 ms). Staccato 8th chords in the stab, light doubles of the stabs, 8th chords in the finale.
- **Orchestra**: every part through `orch.perform` (articulations by length / given: staccato, marcato, pizzicato,
  tremolo, roll / hit; velocities -> the live dynamics lane, swells / crescendi, long notes started early,
  humanized); `orch.ring` raises the hall on the fermata chords.
- **Choirs**: male (calls, low entries), female (answers, staccato waltz 'ah', high entries), the large chorus (tutti
  harmony), 'oh' choir (soft pads); lines with touch() and block harmony under them, long notes started 70 ms early,
  `instrument.dynamics` lanes for the call / answer extremes.
- **Drums**: `drummer.arrange` style rock (riff chorus 0.82, anthem verse 0.6, anthem2 chorus 0.88, solo 0.9, riff2
  0.92, break 'end' with the final hit) + a 1-beat pickup; finale / summit style ballad, energy 0.95, end hit.
- **Bass**: the riff doubled by hand (an octave down), `bassist.arrange` rock (verse / chorus / solo parts, locked
  to the drummer's kick) and ballad (finale), one `bassist.Memory`.
- **Rhythm guitars**: the written riff as power chords (palm-muted A chugs, strummed 9 / 12 ms, two takes);
  `guitarist.arrange` rock for anthem / anthem2 / solo / finale (`.take(2)` on the right guitar), one Memory.
- **Guitar orchestra**: gtr1 (layered/hero_guitar_heavy, centre) + gtr2 / gtr3 (layered/hero_guitar, left / right):
  the theme with `hero.play` (legato, glides into leaps of a 4th+, vibrato 20-24 ct, echo throws), the harmony a
  diatonic third below / above; the solo through `hero.lead` (guitarist.lead rock, climax=True: bends, slides,
  vibrato, budgeted flash moves).

## Needs from sound design

- Piano: warm, the melody layer centred (hero_piano), C4-C6 melody, left hand C2-E4, velocity 30-110 must speak.
- Choirs: exposed pp answers (female choir at dynamics 0.22-0.35) must not click; the male choir G2-F#4.
- Guitars: gtr1 sustains held peaks (the heavy hero), gtr2/gtr3 slightly lower and to the sides; range E4-E6.
- Orchestra: the film orchestra hall for everything (piano and choirs into the same hall), percussion keys: tam-tam 46,
  cymbal roll 48, crash 49, bass drum 36, triangle 81.

## Revision (A&R round 1)

- **finale / summit** (A&R #1): a film tutti instead of a chord wall - the tune in three octaves (violins1 + flutes
  8va; violins2 + trumpets + gtr1 + choir_f at pitch; cellos + horns + choir_m 8vb; the large chorus harmonizes under
  its top voice). Chords only on violas + the rhythm guitars (+ trombones and bass trombone in the summit), the harmony
  guitars around the tune; basses + bass guitar + timpani below. Removed: tuba (but the last chord), organ, piano 8ths,
  trumpet / horn pads, choir_m root fifths, the cellos' root pedal. gtr1 plays the summit line in unison with violins1.
- **ascent -> riff** (A&R #2): the E major fermata diminuendos (dim shapes, choir lanes to 0.3, softer hit); riff and
  riff2 get the orchestra on the riff accents (trombones power chords, horns 8va, bass trombone, cellos, basses, timpani);
  crash + concert bass drum on the riff downbeat; riff2 adds tremolo violins A5 / E6.
- **Part III layers**: anthem violas / cellos pad (the organ ridden up), anthem2 chorus 'ah' + horns pads, solo the
  orchestra's pads + the chorus for its last 4 bars + a timpani roll and crash into its D6 peak.
- **calls** (A&R #4): horns marcato double the men's upbeat + target note, pizzicato violins1 the women's; the choir
  lines sing at velocity 120 with their written velocities on an expression lane (`speak()` in song.py); the p / pp
  answers +10-12 velocity (A&R #5). The waltz 'ah' goes through the same `speak()`, 0.55 beats long.
- **fall**: violins2 (Ab4 C5 Eb5) + cellos swell at 46-50, no violas (they masked violins2's presence).
- **ballad**: left hand 8 velocity softer (lh_vel 46-54, intro rolled chords 46, coda 40).
