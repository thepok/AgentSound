# Chorale Prelude in F - form

An original hymn tune set as a chorale prelude in two verses (Bach / Pachelbel / Brahms manner), 34 bars of 4/4,
played by `agentsound.organist` on the Burea church organ. Every note is written by hand in `song.py`.

**The tune** (4 phrases of six quarters and a half note, F4-G5), each phrase ending in a different cadence:

| phrase | notes            | cadence                                              |
|--------|------------------|------------------------------------------------------|
| 1      | F G A F, C Bb A  | imperfect authentic (V7 - I, cantus on the third)    |
| 2      | A C D C, Bb A G  | half cadence (ii - I6 - V)                           |
| 3      | C D E F, G E F   | deceptive (V - vi, cadential trill on the leading tone) |
| 4      | D C Bb A, Bb G F | perfect authentic (IV - V - I, cadential trill on G) |

**The motif**: an 8th rest and three stepwise 8ths running into a held half note. The alto runs into every downbeat
while the tenor holds, the tenor runs into every third beat while the alto holds, so the 8ths never stop and each
voice breathes on its rest. The pedal walks in quarters (Subbas 16' + Gedakt 8').

|---------|------|----|--------------|---------|
| verse 1 | 16   | 54 | cantus: Huvudverk cornet (Gedakt 8, Rorflojt 4, Sesquialtera II); accompaniment: Brostverk flutes (Gedakt 8, Koppelflojt 4); pedal Subbas 16 + Gedakt 8 | 3-bar Vorspiel on the motif; the four phrases with mordents, a pralltriller and two cadential trills, a one-bar interlude after phrases 1-3 (the third turns to D minor and its dominant A, resolved deceptively to Bb under phrase 4); a plagal postlude (IV - I), rit., fermata, ~2.3 s of church ring |
| verse 2 | 18   | 50 | cantus: Brostverk Krumhorn 8; accompaniment: swell Salicional 8 (the box breathing with each phrase, open for phrase 3, closing in the coda); same pedal | the same Vorspiel, accompaniment and interludes; the cantus coloured with 16th-note diminutions (every tune note on its beat); a 4-bar coda over a tonic pedal point (ii7/F - V7/F - IV/F - I) where the cantus sings a last G Bb, D C, A; rit., long fermata |

Counterpoint checked by script while writing: no parallel fifths / octaves between any two voices at 8th
resolution, no voice crossings, strong-beat dissonances only as chord sevenths and over the final pedal point.
