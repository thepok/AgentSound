When the Year Turns Gold
(AgentSound - Blue Hour Sessions; sung by Hanami)

[Verse - rubato]
Summer took the early train,
left the sandals in the hall,
and the streets put on their coats
like they never cared at all.

[Refrain]
When the year turns gold -
that was what you said,
by the ferry gate at five,
with the maples red.

When the year turns gold,
down along the shore,
in the coat you used to hate,
like the year before.

[Bridge]
Leaves come down like letters,
none of them from you.
I should have asked you to stay,
but I thought you knew.

When the year turns gold
and the lamps come on,
on the bench out by the gate
till the light is gone.

[Strings and piano]

[Bridge]
Leaves come down like letters,
none of them from you.
I should have asked you to stay,
but I thought you knew.

When the year turns gold
and the gold turns gray,
you still know the ferry gate -
it's not far away.

[Tag]
When the year turns gold,
when the year turns gold.
