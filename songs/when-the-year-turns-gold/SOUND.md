# When the Year Turns Gold - sound (roles/sound-designer.md)

- **Voice**: Hanami (DiffSinger), singer style `ballad`: the soft mode alone (100 % soft, 0 % power), `vel=(56, 84)`,
  `late_ms=58`, `late_first_ms=20` (at 62 BPM the onset consonants run ~105 ms: the P-centre lands +5.5 ms, on the
  beat, 0 % of the syllables early), `mic_pull` on (the style's default: every long phrase end pulled 3-5.7 dB back
  from the mic). `hero(family='vocal', genre='jazz', drive=False)`: her own short plate ~20 LU under her, no echo, no
  throws; the strings duck 1.5 dB and give 3 kHz while she sings. One voice, no double, no harmony.
- **Piano**: the `jazz_ballad` preset's Salamander grand (soft hammers, warm, no presence boost) on two tracks -
  `piano` (melody, fills) and `comp` (rolled chords, the verse's left-hand roots) -, the pedal with the harmony.
- **Bass**: the Meatbass upright pizz, two-feel, long notes (gate 0.95). **Brushes**: the Swirly kit, stirs only, no
  kick, no fills.
- **Strings**: violins I / II, violas, cellos from `bands.symphony_orchestra` (SSO 4.0 / VPO sections in their own
  concert hall), +9 dB on the faders (their calibrated mf sits ~19 dB under a close voice), the hall return opened to
  20 kHz with +2 dB above 9 kHz (the sections are dark on top). No hero, no doublers, no octave layer on any
  acoustic instrument. **Harp** (SSO): one glissando into B2 and the last chord's roll, +12 dB.
- **Space**: the band's 224XL chamber, the orchestra's hall, the voice's own plate. Reverb ~-16 LU under the mix.
