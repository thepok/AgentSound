# Veranda Light - sound design

Locked by the brief: the endless jazz-piano sound with the bossa feel, unchanged. `song.py` takes it from
`agentsound.endless.perform.band(s, G, 'bossa', jazz.Feel(118, ratio=0.5, layback={'piano': 8, 'comp': 4, ...}))`:
the genre's `solo_piano` preset (the Salamander grand on `piano` / `comp`, room + plate), the genre's master gain and
the bossa feel's -1.5 dB - identical to an endless bossa tune by construction.
