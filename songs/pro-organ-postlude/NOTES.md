# Trumpet Voluntary in D (pro reference: festive postlude)

Hand-composed trumpet voluntary in the English manner (Stanley / Clarke / Boyce), D major, q=88 (echo 86, fugato 92,
finale 84), ~2:38. Played by `agentsound.organist.perform` on the Burea `church_organ`
(registers `trumpet`, `positiv` + `soft`, `principal`, `full`, `pedal`, `pedal_full`).

| section | bars  | registration | content |
|---------|-------|--------------|---------|
| tune    | 1-16  | trumpet (HW) / positiv chords / pedal | dotted tune: 4+4 period in D (half cadence, PAC with a cadential trill), E7 pivot, the tune restated in A, cadence in A (trill); fermata, the church rings |
| echo    | 17-27 | trumpet / positiv echo / soft chords (swell box closes under the echoes) / pedal (silent in the echoes) | 2-bar phrase in A + echo, the same in F# minor + echo, a phrase cadencing in B minor (trill on C#) |
| fugato  | 28-41 | principal + pedal | subject (alto, D), real answer (soprano, A) over a countersubject, subject in the pedal; circle-of-fifths episode with a 7-3 suspension chain (Em7 A7 Dmaj7 G C#o7 F#7 Bm7 E7); 3-bar dominant pedal on A, rising soprano; A7 with fermata |
| finale  | 42-53 | full + pedal_full | the tune on full organ; deceptive cadence (A7 - Bm) instead of the PAC, the tune's 2nd bar again, a climb to D6, IV - ii6 - I6/4 - V7 (trill) - I, ritardando, held final chord with fermata |

Key plan: D - A (dominant) - F#m - Bm - D (fugato) - A pedal - D. The written voices were checked for parallel
fifths / octaves between every pair of voices (melody, chord top / bottom, pedal).
